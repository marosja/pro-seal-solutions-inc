Privacy Policy

Pro Seal Solutions Inc. — Calgary, AB

1. What Personal Information Is Collected

When you submit a quote request through this website, we collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Project location
  • Project details (message field)
  • Optional uploaded project photos
  • Technical anti-spam data required to process the form (Cloudflare Turnstile)

2. Why the Information Is Collected

We collect this information to:

  • Respond to your quote request
  • Discuss your project and provide a service estimate
  • Follow up on your inquiry
  • Protect the website from spam and automated abuse

3. How the Information Is Used

Submitted information is sent by email to our team. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. Your information is not sold, shared with third parties for marketing purposes, or used for any purpose other than responding to your specific request.

4. Third-Party Services

This website may use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile — used for spam protection on the quote form. Cloudflare may process form submission data to verify you are a real user. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Transactional email provider — used to deliver quote request emails to our inbox. The specific provider will be disclosed once selected.
  • Hosting provider — standard server logs may be processed by the website hosting provider.

No analytics or tracking tools are enabled at launch. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, this policy will be updated and a cookie notice will be implemented.

5. Cookies

At launch, this website does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or tracking technologies. Cloudflare Turnstile may set functional cookies required for spam protection. These are not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

No cookie consent banner is required at launch because no tracking or analytics cookies are used. If analytics or advertising is added in the future, a cookie notice will be added before enabling those tools.

6. Data Retention

  • Quote request emails are retained in the company email inbox.
  • Uploaded photos, if stored temporarily for delivery, are deleted after 30–90 days unless required for an active quote or project.
  • We do not maintain a separate customer database for the MVP website.

  • Form input is validated and sanitized before processing.
  • Uploaded files are validated and restricted to safe image and document formats.
  • API keys and credentials are stored securely and not exposed in frontend code.
