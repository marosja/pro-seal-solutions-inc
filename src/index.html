What We Do

Epoxy Flooring, Concrete Coatings & Surface Restoration Services

Pro Seal Solutions Inc. specializes in epoxy flooring, concrete coatings, repair, resurfacing, floor leveling, and surface preparation for residential and commercial properties in Calgary and surrounding areas. We improve existing concrete surfaces with durable, clean, and long-lasting finishes.

Epoxy Flooring & Floor Coatings

Durable, low-maintenance coating systems that protect concrete and create a clean, polished finish for garages, shops, basements, warehouses, and commercial spaces.

Concrete Repair & Restoration

Repair solutions for cracked, chipped, worn, or damaged concrete surfaces to restore safety, strength, usability, and appearance before coating, resurfacing, or continued use.

Concrete Resurfacing & Overlays

Refreshing worn or aging concrete with a new surface layer to improve appearance, usability, and durability without full replacement when the existing surface allows it.

Self-Leveling Concrete & Floor Leveling

Creating smoother, more even concrete surfaces for basements, garages, commercial spaces, renovations, coatings, and flooring preparation.

Concrete Surface Preparation

Grinding, cleaning, and preparing existing concrete surfaces for coating, repair, resurfacing, leveling, or restoration work.
