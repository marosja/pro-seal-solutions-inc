Primary goal:
Build a fast, responsive, one-page marketing website focused on epoxy flooring, concrete coatings, concrete repair, resurfacing, floor leveling, and concrete surface preparation in Calgary, Alberta.

Important:
The company does not pour new concrete. The company focuses on epoxy flooring, coatings, repair, resurfacing, leveling, restoration, and surface preparation after concrete is already laid.
