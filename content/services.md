## 5. Concrete Surface Preparation

### Service Summary

Grinding, cleaning, and preparing concrete surfaces for repair, resurfacing, leveling, or coating.

### Expanded Description

Proper surface preparation is one of the most important steps for successful concrete repair, resurfacing, floor leveling, and coating installation. Pro Seal Solutions Inc. prepares existing concrete surfaces so that the next layer or finish can bond properly and perform as expected.

Surface preparation may include grinding, cleaning, removing weak surface material, opening the concrete profile, addressing surface contamination, and preparing the floor for epoxy, overlays, self-leveling products, or repair materials.

Skipping preparation can lead to poor adhesion, coating failure, uneven finishes, and shorter service life. For this reason, surface preparation should be treated as a core part of the service, not an optional extra.

### Best For

- Floors being prepared for epoxy coating
- Concrete before resurfacing
- Concrete before self-leveling
- Worn or contaminated surfaces
- Garage floors before coating
- Commercial floors before restoration
- Floors needing grinding or cleaning before repair

### Key Benefits

- Supports better adhesion for coatings and overlays
- Helps improve the quality of the final finish
- Removes weak or unsuitable surface material
- Prepares concrete for long-lasting results
- Reduces risk of coating or resurfacing failure
- Important for both residential and commercial projects
