## Required Topics

The privacy policy must cover the following:

### 1. What Personal Information Is Collected

The quote request form collects:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Project location
- Project details (free-text message)
- Optional uploaded project photos
- Technical data required for spam protection (Cloudflare Turnstile token)

### 2. Why the Information Is Collected

- To respond to the customer's quote request.
- To discuss the customer's project.
- To provide a service estimate or follow-up.
- To protect the website from spam and abuse.

### 3. How the Information Is Used

- Submitted information is sent by email to the company.
- The company uses the information only to respond to the inquiry and discuss the project.
- Information is not sold, shared, or used for marketing beyond the specific quote request.

### 4. Third-Party Services

The website may use the following third-party services:

- **Cloudflare Turnstile** — for spam protection on the quote form.
- **Transactional email provider** — for delivering form submissions to the company inbox (provider to be determined during implementation).
- **Hosting provider** — may process standard server logs.

> **Note:** No analytics or tracking tools are enabled at launch. If analytics is added later, this section must be updated and a cookie notice must be added.

### 5. Cookies

At launch, the website does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or tracking technologies.

Cloudflare Turnstile may set functional cookies required for spam protection. These are not used for tracking or advertising.

> **No cookie banner is required at launch.** If analytics or tracking is added later, a cookie notice must be implemented before enabling those tools.

### 6. Data Retention

- Quote request emails are retained in the company inbox.
- Uploaded photos, if stored temporarily for delivery, are deleted after 30–90 days unless needed for an active quote or project.
- The company does not maintain a separate customer database for MVP.

### 7. Customer Rights

Customers may contact the company to:

- Request access to their submitted information.
- Request correction of their submitted information.
- Request deletion of their submitted information.

Contact: michal@prosealsolutions.ca

### 8. Security

- All form submissions are sent over HTTPS.
- Form input is validated and sanitized server-side.
- Uploaded files are validated and restricted to safe image formats.
- API keys and credentials are stored securely and not exposed in frontend code.

### 9. Changes to This Policy

The company may update this privacy policy. Changes will be reflected on this page.
